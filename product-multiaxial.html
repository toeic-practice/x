Multiaxial Fabric

Multi-directional carbon fiber reinforcement with multiple
fiber-orientation and layer-weight configurations.

Product Overview

The FTC catalogue lists multiaxial carbon fabrics in several fiber-orientation configurations, including ±45°, 0°/90°, +45°/90°/-45°, 0°/+45°/-45°, and 0°/+45°/90°/-45°.

Listed fiber weight per layer ranges from 75 to 300 g/m², while total fabric weight ranges from 157 to 907 g/m².

All listed products have a 127 cm width. Roll length is 50 m for ECMT products and 30 m for ECMF products shown in the catalogue.
