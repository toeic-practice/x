Prepreg

UD carbon fiber prepreg with multiple weight, resin content
and carbon yarn configurations.

Product Overview

The FTC catalogue lists UD carbon-fiber prepreg in six areal-weight options from 75 to 250 g/m².

All listed prepreg options use a resin-content range of 30–40% and a Tg range of 120–250°C, with 100 cm width.

Carbon-yarn options include 12K, 12K/24K and 24K depending on product weight. Other specifications can be customized based on request.
